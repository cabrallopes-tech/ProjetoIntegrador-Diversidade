Special spotlight • Brazil

Chico Mendes

1944 – 1988 | Environmentalist and union leader

Francisco Alves Mendes Filho, known as Chico Mendes, was a Brazilian rubber tapper, union leader, and environmentalist. He defended the preservation of the Amazon Rainforest and the rights of the workers who depended on it to survive.

He organized the so-called empates (standoffs), peaceful movements carried out by rubber tappers to prevent deforestation. His struggle gained international recognition, but he was murdered in 1988 for his work defending the forest and local communities.

"At first I thought I was fighting to save rubber trees, then I thought I was fighting to save the Amazon Rainforest. Now I realize I am fighting for humanity."

Quote attributed to Chico Mendes.
